## ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS

### Why Scores Are Low

**1. Missing Supporting Images (CRITICAL - 10/10 posts affected)**
- **Current state:** 0 images per post
- **Target state:** 4-6 supporting images per post
- **Impact on score:** -6 to -9 points per post
- **Why it happened:** Posts were created with hero images only; supporting content images not added
- **Fix:** Add 4-6 images per post showing concepts, diagrams, workflows, comparisons

**2. Missing or Weak Citations (8/10 posts affected)**
- **Current state:** Most posts have 0 citations (only Parts 1 & 2 reference sources inline)
- **Target state:** 8+ credible source mentions per post
- **Impact on score:** -5 to -8 points per post
- **Why it happened:** Content written from expert knowledge without formal citations
- **Fix:** Add inline citations, reference real data, link to authoritative sources

**3. Missing FAQ Sections (6/10 posts affected)**
- **Current state:** Only Parts 1 & 2 have FAQ sections
- **Target state:** All posts should have FAQ for AI citation readiness
- **Impact on score:** -3 to -5 points per post
- **Why it happened:** FAQ was treated as optional rather than standard
- **Fix:** Add FAQ section to each post with 5-7 common questions

**4. Weak Internal Linking (10/10 posts affected)**
- **Current state:** Average 1.1 internal links per post
- **Target state:** 5+ internal links (series navigation, related topics)
- **Impact on score:** -2 to -4 points per post
- **Why it happened:** Series navigation not systematically added; cross-references minimal
- **Fix:** Add previous/next post links, related series links, intra-series references

**5. Some Posts Under 1500 Words (4/10 posts affected)**
- **Current state:** Average 1444 words (Parts 5, 6, 9 below 1300)
- **Target state:** 1500-2500 words optimal
- **Impact on score:** -1 to -3 points per post
- **Why it happened:** Content focused on conciseness; depth could improve
- **Fix:** Expand with more examples, case studies, implementation details
